    About

    Building pipelines
    that hold up.

    I'm a London-based data engineer who started out as an analyst, so I know what it means to depend on the numbers at the end of a pipeline. Since then I've cut data freshness from 6 hours to 45 minutes at Somalis in Tech, earned a First Class BSc in Data Science & Computing from Birkbeck, University of London, and spent most of 2026 delivering 10+ client data platforms as a freelancer.

    I care most about the unglamorous parts that make data dependable: tests, lineage, freshness SLAs, and documentation. Right now I'm finishing the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate certification and building new projects, working with data from transport, sport, finance, and the environment.
